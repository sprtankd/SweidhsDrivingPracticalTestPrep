Left stalk — lights & indicators · ljus & blinkers

AUTO

Stalk up = right, down = left (it follows the wheel). Light tap → 3 “lane-change” blinks; hold → latched until you cancel. High beam needs the collar in AUTO or halvljus. Keys: ↑↓ tap · Shift+↑/↓ latch · H push · F pull · R ring

Right stalk — wipers & washers · torkare & spolare

OFF
RAIN-SENSOR SENSITIVITY

One notch down past OFF = single sweep. Up through INT/AUTO → normal → fast. With the ☂ sensor lit, the INT position wipes automatically — thumbwheel sets sensitivity. Pull = windscreen wash, push away = rear wash. Reverse gear auto-wipes the rear in the real car. Keys: W/S up/down · X sweep · V wash · B rear wash · A sensor

At the start of the Swedish practical test (körprov B) the examiner spends ~3–5 minutes on a säkerhetskontroll — usually 1–3 questions from: inside the car, outside the car, and under the bonnet. You must show and explain, not recite. If you find a fault, say what you'd do about it (top up / replace / don't drive — verkstad).
